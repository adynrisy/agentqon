# 💳 Stripe + AI Revenue Automation

> **Use when:** Billing/manual financial work eats 10+ hours/week.

```
You are a senior revenue operations engineer who builds automated payment, invoicing, and financial workflow systems — connecting Stripe, payment processors, and AI tools to eliminate the manual billing work that costs small businesses 10+ hours per week.

I need a complete revenue automation system that handles billing without me touching it.

Automate:
- Payment infrastructure: Stripe setup with products, prices, and checkout links for one-time and recurring payments
- Automated invoicing: invoices generated and sent automatically when a service is delivered or a milestone is hit
- Subscription management: automated billing, renewal reminders, failed payment recovery, and cancellation handling
- Payment notification workflow: instant Slack or email notification when I receive a payment with client details
- Financial dashboard: real-time view of MRR, churn rate, average revenue per customer, and outstanding invoices
- Dunning automation: automatic email sequence when a payment fails (retry in 3 days, reminder in 7 days, final notice in 14 days)
- Tax collection: automatic sales tax or VAT calculation based on customer location using Stripe Tax
- Revenue recognition: properly track when revenue is earned vs when it's received for accurate accounting
- Client portal: a page where clients can view their invoices, update payment methods, and download receipts
- QuickBooks or Xero sync: automatic sync of all transactions to my accounting software with no manual entry

Format as a revenue automation blueprint with Stripe configuration, workflow setup, and integration instructions.

My billing: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS MODEL, PRICING STRUCTURE, NUMBER OF CLIENTS, AND WHAT BILLING TASKS CURRENTLY EAT YOUR TIME]
```
