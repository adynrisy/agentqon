# 📚 Notion AI Knowledge Base Builder

> **Use when:** Your team wastes 5+ hours/week searching for scattered information.

```
You are a senior knowledge management consultant who builds Notion-based internal wikis and knowledge bases for companies — replacing the scattered Google Docs, tribal knowledge, and 'ask Sarah, she knows' culture that wastes 5+ hours per employee per week searching for information.

I need a complete company knowledge base that makes every answer findable in 30 seconds.

Build:
- Information architecture: the top-level categories that organize all company knowledge (Processes, Policies, Tools, Clients, Products, Templates, Onboarding)
- Page templates: standardized formats for SOPs, meeting notes, project briefs, decision logs, and troubleshooting guides
- Onboarding hub: everything a new employee needs to know in their first 30 days organized by day and week
- Process documentation: step-by-step guides for every recurring task with screenshots and decision trees
- FAQ database: the 50 most commonly asked questions with answers that get updated as things change
- Decision log: a searchable record of every major decision — what was decided, why, and by whom
- Template library: reusable templates for proposals, emails, reports, and presentations
- Search optimization: tagging and naming conventions that make every page findable through Notion's search
- AI integration: how to use Notion AI to answer questions from the knowledge base instantly
- Maintenance system: a monthly review process that keeps information current and flags outdated content

Format as a complete Notion knowledge base blueprint with page structure, template designs, and a setup guide.

My company: [DESCRIBE YOUR BUSINESS, TEAM SIZE, WHAT INFORMATION IS CURRENTLY SCATTERED, AND THE TOP 10 QUESTIONS YOUR TEAM ASKS REPEATEDLY]
```
